Questions people ask

Numerology calculator FAQs

How do I calculate my Mulank (driver number)?

Add the digits of the day you were born until you get a single digit. Born on the 23rd: 2 + 3 = 5, so your Mulank is 5. The calculator on this page does it instantly.

How do I calculate my Bhagyank (conductor or destiny number)?

Add every digit of your full date of birth and reduce to a single digit. For 23-08-1994: 2+3+0+8+1+9+9+4 = 36, and 3+6 = 9, so the Bhagyank is 9.

What is the Lo Shu grid and what are missing numbers?

The Lo Shu grid is a 3×3 magic square (4-9-2, 3-5-7, 8-1-6). The digits of your birth date are placed in it; numbers that never appear are your missing numbers, which numerology links to qualities you may need to develop consciously.

How do I know if my mobile number is lucky?

Add all ten digits of the number and reduce to one digit, then compare it with your Mulank. Numbers ruled by planets friendly to your Mulank planet are considered supportive. A full check also looks at digit pairs and repetitions.

Which system does the name number calculator use?

The Chaldean system, widely used in Indian numerology, where each letter carries a value from 1 to 8 based on its sound vibration.
