How much does numerology name correction cost?

Name correction at Spirit Stars is ₹905. Mobile, vehicle and house number suggestions are ₹905, date-of-birth analysis is ₹1,400, and a new-born baby name recommendation or a complete numerology consultation is ₹2,300.

Does changing the spelling of my name really help?

Numerology holds that the vibration of your name should support your date-of-birth numbers. A small spelling change can bring the two into harmony; Acharya ji suggests changes only where they are genuinely needed.

How is a lucky mobile number chosen in numerology?

The digits of the number are reduced and compared with your birth number and destiny number. You receive a short list of combinations that suit you, which you can use when buying a new SIM or vehicle.

What details are needed for a baby name recommendation?

The baby's date, time and place of birth, the family surname and any letters you prefer. Names are suggested to suit both numerology and the birth chart.
