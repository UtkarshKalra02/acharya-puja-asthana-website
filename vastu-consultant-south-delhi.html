Is online Vastu consultation effective?

Yes, when you share an accurate floor plan with the north direction marked. Acharya ji analyses the plan, marks the Vastu grid and suggests remedies over Zoom. A site visit adds a reading of the actual energy and layout on the ground.

Can Vastu doshas be fixed without demolition?

In most cases, yes. Remedies such as rearranging furniture and functions, element balancing, colours and directional corrections are tried first. Structural changes are suggested only when truly necessary.

What is Astro-Vastu?

Astro-Vastu combines your birth chart with the Vastu of your space, so remedies are personalised to the people living or working there rather than applied generically.

Why is Vastu important for a factory or showroom?

Business owners use Vastu to plan the placement of entrances, machinery, cash, stores and the owner's seat so the space supports smooth operations and growth. The factory consultation includes a site visit.

What is a Vastu gridding map?

A gridding map marks the 16 Vastu directions and zones on your floor plan, showing exactly which activity sits in which zone. It is the foundation for every Vastu correction.
