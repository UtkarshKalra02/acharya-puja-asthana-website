Astrology FAQs

What is the difference between Vedic and KP astrology?

Vedic (Parashari) astrology reads the whole birth chart through signs, houses, planets and dashas to describe life themes and periods. KP (Krishnamurti Paddhati) astrology uses star-lords and sub-lords for sharper yes/no answers and event timing. Acharya Puja Asthana uses both, choosing the method that best fits your question.

How much does an astrology consultation cost in Delhi at Spirit Stars?

What is Prashna astrology and when is it used?

Prashna (horary) astrology answers a specific question using the chart of the moment the question is asked. It is useful when your birth time is unknown or when you need guidance on one urgent matter.

Is kundli matching necessary before marriage?

Many families use kundli matching to understand compatibility, temperament and timing. Beyond guna scores, Acharya ji looks at the full charts of both partners, so the advice is practical rather than a simple pass or fail.

What is medical astrology?

Medical astrology studies the chart for periods that call for extra care with health. It is guidance only and always works alongside — never instead of — advice from your doctor.

Can astrology predict the right time for a job change or business launch?

Astrology can show favourable and challenging periods through dashas and transits, and muhurat selection helps pick an auspicious date. Decisions remain yours; astrology helps you time and prepare for them.
